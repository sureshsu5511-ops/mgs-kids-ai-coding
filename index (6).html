Safe by design

Kids first. Technology second.

The course guide emphasizes parent consent, privacy, supervised AI use, age rules and healthy screen habits.

🔐 Privacy

Never share addresses, phone numbers, school IDs, OTPs or passwords. Use fake data for practice.

👨‍👩‍👧 Supervision

Parents/teachers control online accounts and publishing for younger students.

🤖 Responsible AI

Verify important facts, disclose AI assistance and discuss fairness, copyright and deepfakes.

👀 Screen Health

Use the 20-20-20 rule and balance screen learning with hands-on activities.
